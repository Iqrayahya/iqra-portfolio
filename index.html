About me

Who I am

I'm an independent AI creator and builder. Half of my work is creative and half is technical, and most clients need both.

On the creative side I script, animate and edit short-form video and ads, built for Reels and TikTok. Every frame, voiceover and caption is made to keep people watching to the end.

On the technical side I build AI agents and automations with Claude Code: WhatsApp assistants that book appointments, research pipelines that find your next content idea, and workflows that take admin off your plate.

Every project goes through an approval step before production starts, so you see the plan before anything is spent.

Questions

Good to know

Who do you work with?

Founders, coaches and business owners who want more attention online and less manual work behind the scenes. I have built for real estate, dental clinics, e-commerce and SaaS.

Do I need to be on camera?

No. Most of my reels are fully animated with motion graphics and AI voiceover. If you want to appear, I can edit your own footage too.

What does an AI agent actually do for my business?

It handles repeat conversations for you: answering common questions, qualifying leads and booking appointments, often on WhatsApp. You stay in control and can step in at any time.

How long does a project take?

A single reel usually takes a few days after the script is approved. Agents and automations depend on scope, and I give you a timeline with the plan in step 02.
